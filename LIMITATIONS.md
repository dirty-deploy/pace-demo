# Limitations (for judges' questions)

## Known issues in the prototype
- The DILR set cap runs in real time (5 to 8 minutes), so in the demo we resume a set we started earlier.
- On a phone, the credit inside the test window shows only when the question palette is opened.
- Quick practice in vocabulary and lesson questions counts towards the streak but adds no study minutes.

## By design in this concept
- No backend and no login: your data stays in this browser, and you can export or import it from Settings.
- Other students' activity (option percentages, community answers, discussions, alum notes) is sample data and is labelled "Sample data".
- The percentile is a rough estimate from a sample score table, not real CAT data.
- Difficulty labels and expected times are estimates from solver effort, not yet checked against real students' times.
- All content is original and small: 2 full mocks, 9 sectionals, topic tests, 6 lessons.
- There is no AI: recommendations come from fixed rules.
