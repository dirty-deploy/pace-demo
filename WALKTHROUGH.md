# Two-minute walkthrough

Open the site at laptop width.

1. **Landing (0:00).** Scroll slowly through the four chapters, or click 01 to 04. Say: "Pace adds decision practice on top of a prep platform like Prepito: choosing, capping, replaying and planning. This is a concept, built on original puzzles." Click **Start today's plan**.
2. **Today (0:25).** Point at "29 days to CAT". Click 30, then 60 minutes. Say: "The plan is sized from how much of the last five sessions the student actually finished, so 60 minutes becomes 48, and anything that doesn't fit is named as dropped." Click **Start**.
3. **Set-pick drill (0:50).** Drag one slab and use an arrow button on another. Say: "Five previews, four minutes, no difficulty shown. The glow shows your rank, not difficulty." Click **Submit order**.
4. **Result (1:05).** Say: "Plain words first: how many of 10 pairs agreed with the solver's estimate. One reason per set, and every number is labelled as an estimate." Click **Try the top set**.
5. **Capped attempt (1:15).** Answer one question and tick "I'm on this clue" on a clue. Say: "The cap comes from the student's own pace. At the cap they choose: leave, or take two more minutes, once." Wait for the cap (about 6 to 9 minutes), or tell the audience what the dialog says and click the close button, then **Leave this set**.
6. **Replay (1:35).** Use the arrow keys on the carousel. Say: "Ten minutes, nothing to type. Where the time went, the start versus the recommended start, where they stalled, and exactly three redo items with dates." Click **Add to today's plan**, then **See today's plan**.
7. **Today again (1:50).** Say: "The redo items are now scheduled, and tomorrow's plan resizes again from what was finished." Point at the footer: "Concept by Team Apex, not affiliated with Prepito."
