# Three-minute walkthrough

Use a fresh browser profile, or Settings → Reset demo first. Timings are a guide.

**0:00 The idea (20 s).** Open `/`. "Pace is our concept redesign of the student experience of Prepito, an independent CAT platform. The flow is familiar on purpose. The design, the copy and every question are ours." Point to the credit footer.

**0:20 Getting started (20 s).** Click **Start preparing**, show step 1, then go back and click **Explore with a demo profile**. "A real student answers four short questions. The demo profile gives us a week of history."

**0:40 Home (25 s).** "This fixes the clutter. Only what matters today: the plan, sized to the time you have, the streak and the one thing to continue." Click **change** and pick 30 minutes to show the plan resize. Everything else has its own page, in clear groups.

**1:05 Daily set and test window (35 s).** Open **Daily set**. Pick 5 questions, 10 minutes and a topic, and read the live summary. Click **Start set**. "This is the exam experience: timer always visible, palette with a legend, mark for review, type-in answers." Open a QA question to show the calculator. Answer one and submit.

**1:40 Review (40 s).** Go to **Review** and open **Full mock 1**, then the **Questions** tab. Show the option bars and the **Sample data** tag: "We label anything that imitates other students." Show the named trap in the explanation, then **Community**. Click a dotted word to open the card: the simpler word, the sentence with it swapped in, and **Add to my words**. Switch to the **Replay** tab on **DILR sectional 1**: where the time went, the cap, the clue where progress stopped, and three redo items.

**2:20 Learn (15 s).** Open a lesson. Press space. "No video files. Slides and transcript run on one clock, the line highlights, and you can click any line."

**2:35 Plan and progress (20 s).** Open **Planner** and type `RC tomorrow 8:30 30m`. Show the preview, press Enter. "Two interactions at most." Open **Streak** to show the rule and calendar.

**2:55 Rewards (5 s).** Open **Rewards** and show coins turning into a Meridian Learning discount. "A proposal, and clearly labelled as illustrative."

## If something goes wrong

- Settings → Reset demo clears everything.
- If the state looks odd, reload. Progress is saved in the browser.
